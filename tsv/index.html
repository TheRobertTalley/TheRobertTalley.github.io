TalleySoft Vision

I wanted thermal, night vision, drone video and a team map in the headset, with my surroundings still visible and my hands free.

TSV adds those views to Quest 3 passthrough. I use external cameras, a powered USB hub and tracked hand controls.

What it does

Thermal & night vision
Overlay either camera, or both, on the forward view. Each camera has its own alignment and image settings. Night vision can switch on automatically when it gets dark.
FPV & camera views
Watch drone video through Sphere Link or the supported WFB receiver. Move FPV between a large view, center, mirror, small HUD and hidden. The weapon-camera viewport stays separate from observation cameras. The rear view can show a selected camera.
Map & Meshtastic
See GPS position, team nodes, messages, markers and routes. The browser operations map connects to headsets for map work and messaging through a configured radio.
Hand controls
Hold a pinch and rotate your wrist to change a view. Triple-tap the opposite wrist to open Settings. Adjust cameras and save the setup without controllers.

Still being worked on

FPV, thermal and night vision have run on the headset. The complete setup is still in testing. Camera alignment during head movement, USB reconnects and FPV reliability need more work.

Radar and visual detection remain experimental. Optical zoom, split-screen thermal/night vision and camera-view head tracking are not ready.

Use TSV

You’ll need a Quest 3, the TSV app and supported hardware for the views you want to use. Local cameras and hand controls run on the headset; team and remote features need their respective connections.
